About me

I’m a designer and video editor who loves making things that look good, feel fun, and actually work. I’m always experimenting with new creative tools, styles, animations, and ideas — basically, if there’s a button I haven’t pressed yet, I’m probably going to press it. 🫠

I’m still building my skills and portfolio, but I’ve already spent a lot of time creating designs, editing videos, and turning random ideas into actual projects. I enjoy learning by doing, trying things that probably shouldn’t work, and somehow making them work anyway.

I like creative projects, clear ideas, cool visuals, and having the freedom to experiment. Away from the screen, you’ll probably find me messing around with technology, working on my next random project, or redesigning something that was perfectly fine five minutes ago. 🚀
